About

I start with your business, not the technology.

Burdenless AI is one person, on purpose. You work with me directly from the first call to the finished workflow.

Who I am

I'm Jeff Donovan. I have spent 20 years in sales, and I have helped businesses build AI workflows their teams use every day.

Sales teaches you to listen before you recommend anything. That is how I approach this work. I want to understand how your business runs and where your people lose time before I suggest a single tool.

Why I started Burdenless AI

The businesses that could gain the most from AI are the ones with nobody on staff to set it up. Large companies have whole departments for this. A business with ten or fifty people has an owner who is already doing three jobs.

I started Burdenless AI to be that missing person: someone who knows the tools, learns your business, and builds what actually saves your team time.

How I work

  • I start with the people doing the work, not with the software.
  • I build on the tools you already pay for.
  • A person on your team approves anything that goes to a customer.
  • You own everything I build. There is no lock-in.
