What we do

Burdenless AI is your office. Built for one person.

Every call answered. Every emergency flagged.

The AI answers your inbound calls when you can't pick up. It captures the job details, understands urgency, books appointments within your guidelines, and texts you immediately for anything that can't wait. No voicemail. No lost jobs.

Tomorrow's schedule, sorted tonight.

Every evening, review tomorrow in one screen. Assign each job to the right tech, set or move dates, and lock the plan in minutes. Your crew sees their day in the app, so there's no dispatch call in the morning.

On-site AI in every tech's pocket.

Open a job and the app briefs the tech on what they're walking into: customer, scope, and your notes for the visit. Out in the field they can ask it anything, how to service the unit, what a part does, what to check next, and get an answer on the spot. No call to the office.

Close the job from the truck.

Your techs tap one button when the job is done. The invoice goes to the customer, a job summary comes to you, the parts used come out of inventory automatically, the Google review request fires, and the 30-day follow-up is scheduled. All of it, from the field. No office needed.
