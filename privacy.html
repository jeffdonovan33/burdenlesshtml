Privacy policy

Privacy policy

Last updated November 16, 2026.

The short version

This website does not use tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or analytics. We only receive information that you choose to send us.

Information you give us

  • When you book a call. Booking runs on Google Calendar. You provide your name and email address, and Google sends both of us a calendar invitation. Google's own privacy policy applies to that service.
  • When you email us. We receive your email address and whatever you write.

How we use it

We use your information to reply to you, to hold the call you booked, and to do the work you hire us for. We do not sell it, rent it, or share it with anyone for marketing.

Services this site relies on

  • Render hosts this website and, like any web host, keeps standard server logs such as IP addresses.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typeface, which means your browser requests font files from Google when a page loads.
  • Google Calendar handles booking, as described above.

Client information

If you become a client, the information we see while working in your systems stays in your accounts. We do not reuse it for any other client, and we do not keep copies after the work ends unless you ask us to.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete any information we hold about you. Email jeff@burdenlessai.com and we will take care of it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
